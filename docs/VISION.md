# Vision

NCFA College Track & Field 27 should feel like a major college-sports career game built around track & field rather than a lightweight track minigame.

The standard is not just realistic lap times. The game should combine:

- believable competition mechanics
- athlete development
- college life and eligibility
- recruiting and scholarships
- meet scoring
- championship qualification
- team identity and rivalries
- broadcast-scale presentation
- long-term records and legacy

The browser requirement is part of the product identity: open the game from a URL and enter a full 3D college track world without installing a native client.
