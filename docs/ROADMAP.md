# NCFA College Track & Field 27 — Production Roadmap

## North star
Build the deepest browser-based collegiate track & field career game possible: believable racing, meaningful training and academics, full meet scoring, recruiting, dynasty management, championship qualification, and broadcast-style presentation.

## Phase 1 — Race foundation
- 800m playable vertical slice
- Effort/pacing controls
- Drafting
- Fatigue model
- Lane movement and basic boxing
- AI race plans
- Live timing, placing and splits
- Chase camera
- Official results

## Phase 2 — Running events
- 60m / 100m / 200m / 400m
- 60H / 100H / 110H / 400H
- 800m / 1500m / mile / 3K / 5K / 10K
- 4x100 / 4x400 / 4x800 / DMR
- Indoor 200m-track geometry and banked turns
- Cross-country courses, terrain and pack racing

## Phase 3 — Field events
- Long jump / triple jump
- High jump
- Pole vault
- Shot put / discus / hammer / weight throw / javelin
- Event-specific approach, timing and release mechanics
- Legal/foul attempt logic
- Flight/result measurement

## Phase 4 — Career life
- Athlete creator
- High-school recruiting profile
- Scholarship offers and commitment
- Dorm/campus hub presentation
- Daily calendar
- Classes, GPA and eligibility
- Practice plans
- Strength training
- Treatment/recovery
- Sleep and readiness
- Coach trust
- Depth charts and relay selection

## Phase 5 — Seasons and championships
- Indoor schedule
- Outdoor schedule
- Cross country for distance athletes
- Invitational meets
- Conference championships
- NCFA first rounds / preliminaries
- NCFA national championship
- Team scoring
- Qualifying marks and rankings
- PR / SB / school / conference / NCFA records
- All-American awards

## Phase 6 — Recruiting and Dynasty
- Full school rosters
- Generated recruiting classes
- Scouting
- Scholarship allocation
- Transfer portal
- Coaching staff
- Facilities
- Program prestige
- Athlete development and retention
- Multi-season simulation
- Team rankings and national titles

## Phase 7 — Presentation
- Broadcast packages
- Meet intros
- Athlete walkouts
- Call-room sequences
- Starting commands
- Crowd and teammate reactions
- Replay cameras
- Photo finish
- Stadium videoboards
- PA announcements
- Storyline overlays

## Phase 8 — Browser scale and online
- Asset streaming
- WebGPU renderer option
- Performance presets
- Gamepad support
- Save profiles
- Cloud saves
- Online races
- Online meets
- Custom schools and rosters
- Shareable meet setups

## Design rule
Every system should support the fantasy of being a college track & field athlete or coach. Campus-life systems should affect performance and career progression without overwhelming the competition itself.
