# NCFA College Track & Field 27 — Core Game Design

## Player fantasy
The player should feel like a real collegiate track & field athlete or head coach inside a fictional national college-sports ecosystem.

## Core modes

### Career
Create one athlete and live a four-year college career.

Main systems:
- recruiting and scholarship offers
- school choice
- academics and eligibility
- daily schedule
- event-group practice
- strength and recovery
- coach trust
- depth charts and relay pools
- indoor, outdoor and cross-country seasons
- conference and NCFA championships
- transfer portal
- records, awards and pro decision

### Dynasty
Control a full track & field program.

Main systems:
- recruiting
- scholarships
- roster construction
- event-group coaches
- facilities
- training philosophy
- meet scheduling
- athlete development
- transfers
- team scoring
- conference and national championships

### Quick Meet
Choose an event, venue and athletes and compete immediately.

### Championship
Play a focused postseason route from conference championships through NCFA nationals.

## Career archetypes

### Sprint Career
60m, 100m, 200m, 400m, hurdles and relays.

Primary ratings:
- reaction
- acceleration
- maximum velocity
- speed endurance
- hurdle technique
- relay exchange skill

### Distance Career
Cross country, 800m, 1500m, mile, 3K, 5K and 10K.

Primary ratings:
- aerobic capacity
- running economy
- lactate tolerance
- speed reserve
- tactical awareness
- kick

### Jumps Career
High jump, long jump, triple jump and pole vault.

Primary ratings:
- approach consistency
- elastic power
- speed
- takeoff timing
- event technique

### Throws Career
Shot put, discus, hammer, weight throw and javelin.

Primary ratings:
- strength
- rotational speed
- release speed
- release angle control
- event technique

### Combined Events
Pentathlon, heptathlon and decathlon.

Primary ratings:
- versatility
- fatigue resistance
- technical breadth
- recovery
- consistency

## Race design principles

### No arcade stamina bar driving everything
Fatigue should emerge from effort, fitness, event duration and prior workload. UI can communicate effort and condition, but the athlete's movement and responsiveness should also show fatigue.

### Position matters
Middle-distance and distance racing should model:
- drafting
- rail position
- moving wide
- getting boxed
- surges
- kicks
- pace changes

### Sprinting is phase-based
Sprint races should distinguish:
- reaction
- drive phase
- transition
- maximum velocity
- speed maintenance

### Meets matter
Individual places contribute to team scoring. Career races should affect:
- team points
- athlete ranking
- coach trust
- qualifying status
- records and awards

## College-life loop
A typical career day can include:
1. wake-up/readiness
2. breakfast
3. classes
4. practice
5. lift or treatment
6. study hall/social choices
7. sleep

These systems should resolve quickly and meaningfully rather than becoming a walking simulator.

## Fictional universe
NCFA stands for National Collegiate Federation of Athletics. Schools, conferences, branding and championship properties should remain original unless separately licensed.
